# Wolf Pack Wash — 30-Day Content Plan
## Strategy: Stop the Scroll → Build Trust → Drive DMs

**The rule:** Every post has ONE job. Make them stop, make them feel something, make them DM or comment.

**Pillars:**
- 🔥 Transformations (40%) — Before/afters. Nothing converts harder.
- ⭐ Social Proof (20%) — Reviews, results, customer stories
- 💡 Education (20%) — Authority content, why it matters
- 💰 Promotions (10%) — Specific, time-bound, value-stacked offers
- 🐺 Brand (10%) — Personality, local, "this is who we are"

**CTA Rotation (vary this — don't use the same one every post):**
- "Comment 'QUOTE' and we'll DM you"
- "DM us BEFORE for a free quote"
- "Tag a neighbor whose driveway needs this"
- "Save this post — you'll need it"
- "Link in bio to book your spot"
