## Wolf Pack Wash reactivation planning session

- User wants Wolf Pack Wash reactivation built **before** further ad optimization work.
- Initial generic reactivation/email plan using a broad $199-style angle was rejected as off-target because this campaign is for **past customers**, not cold/new leads.
- Correct strategic direction established: build a **past-customer rebooking campaign** centered on getting prior good customers back on the schedule.
- User emphasized that these customers already have prior pricing history, so campaign should lean on that continuity instead of using a fresh acquisition-style promo.

### Critical risk constraint
- Some customers received a duplicate charge months after service last year.
- User believes some may not have directly correlated it with Wolf Pack Wash, but wants to be extremely careful with reactivation outreach because of this risk.
- This makes segmentation/list hygiene mandatory before any send.

### Pricing/offer direction
- User wants to lean on a retention angle like: keep the same rate from last year.
- Strong message direction discussed: "Last year we washed your house at the address. If you want to get back on the schedule, click here to keep your same rate from last year."
- Final wording not locked yet, but pricing strategy choice is to leverage **same-rate-from-last-year** positioning rather than generic promotional pricing.

### Channel strategy
- User wants a full **SMS + email** funnel, not just email.
- Sequence discussed:
  - Day 1 SMS blast
  - Day 2 follow-up SMS to non-responders / non-clickers
  - Day 3 email
  - further follow-up email/SMS touches after that
- User wants link tracking and downstream conversion tracking across the whole reactivation flow.

### Booking / landing experience direction
- Initial thought was a preference-only CTA page where customers choose preferred day/time and then receive a follow-up confirmation.
- Direction then evolved toward a stronger **calendar-backed slot picker** if Google Calendar integration is available.
- User likes the idea of available slots filling in real time so the schedule appears fuller and double-booking risk is reduced.
- Stronger confirmation experience discussed:
  - customer books from available slots
  - page says: "Thanks, we've confirmed your appointment."
  - ideally supports Add to Calendar button and/or confirmation email/calendar invite

### Sender identity

- User likes using **Robin** as the outbound assistant/scheduler persona for this flow.
- Recommended usage pattern discussed: "Robin with Wolf Pack Wash"
