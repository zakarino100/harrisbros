- `GET /api/reviews/:brand` — fetch all published 5-star reviews for a brand
- `POST /api/reviews/:brand/sync` — manually trigger review sync
- `PATCH /api/reviews/:id/publish` — toggle review visibility
- `GET /embed/reviews/:brand` — self-contained JS widget for website embed
