# Wolf Pack Wash Reactivation Email Plan

## Goal
Wake up prior customers and turn dormant jobs back into booked exterior cleaning work, starting with the easiest/safest segments first.

## Core constraint
Do **not** blast the full old customer list blindly. Because of the prior billing issues/disputes, this campaign should be segmented before send.

## Recommended segmentation

### Segment A — Clean past customers
Send first.
Criteria:
- previously completed job
- no refund / dispute / chargeback
- no major complaint
- no bad Yelp/review recovery issue tied to them
- valid email

### Segment B — Warm but uncertain
Send only after list review.
Criteria:
- completed job but notes are incomplete
- unclear satisfaction status
- old records with weak documentation

### Segment C — Do not email yet
Hold out of initial send.
Criteria:
- refund/dispute/chargeback
- known complaint or service recovery problem
- opted out / DNC / unsubscribe
- invalid or obviously stale email

## Offer strategy
Lead with the same value frame already proven in WPW sales:
- local route-based availability
- simple house wash core offer
- clear seasonal reason to act now
- low-friction CTA to reply or book

Primary offer to test:
- House wash starting at **$199** for homes under 3,000 sqft

Optional secondary mention:
- bundle options available for gutters / driveway / other exterior surfaces

## Tone rules
- plainspoken, local, direct
- not corporate
- no long "we miss you" fluff
- confidence > hype
- acknowledge they used WPW before without sounding automated

# Send strategy

## Initial send size
Do a controlled first send, not full-list:
- first batch: 50 to 150 clean records max
- wait 24 to 48 hours
- review opens, clicks, replies, unsubscribes, spam complaints, bookings
- then expand if healthy

## Best CTA
For now, best CTA is **reply to email**.
Why:
- lower friction
- easier to gauge intent
- safer than forcing a booking flow before list quality is proven

Secondary CTA if needed:
- call/text `(919) 738-5394`

## Best send window
Recommended first test:
- Tuesday to Thursday
- 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM local time

## Smart personalization fields
Use if available:
- first name
- city
- last service type
- last service year

Do **not** over-personalize if records are messy.

# Measurement
Track at minimum:
- delivered
- open rate
- reply rate
- booked jobs
- unsubscribe rate
- spam complaint rate
- revenue per 100 sends

Main KPI:
- booked jobs per 100 clean records emailed

# What to prep before sending
1. Export prior customer list
2. Remove disputes/refunds/problem accounts
3. Remove unsubscribes/DNC/bad emails
4. Split by city/area if possible
5. Load into sender platform
6. Set up reply handling so responses do not sit cold
7. Send small first batch
8. Review results before scaling
