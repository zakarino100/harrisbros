01 — Identity
Logo System
Primary — Dark Background
Inverted — Light Background

Logo Usage Rules

Always maintain clear space equal to the height of the "W" around the logo on all sides
Use on dark backgrounds whenever possible — this is the primary application
Minimum digital size: 120px wide. Minimum print size: 1.25 inches wide
Never stretch, skew, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo
Never place the logo on busy photo backgrounds without a solid color block or dark overlay
Always use vector (SVG/AI/EPS) for print — PNG only for digital use
The wolf mark may be used alone as a brand icon at small sizes (social avatars, favicons)

Color Usage Guide

Accents / CTAs Wolf Red (#B40000) — buttons, highlights, logo accents, borders
Headlines / Body Clean White (#FFFFFF) — all text on dark backgrounds
Price Badges (Ads) Gold (#D4A017) — pricing callouts in Facebook/Instagram ads only
Light Backgrounds Use Pack Black or Wolf Red for text. Avoid gray text on white.

04 — Voice & Tone
Brand Voice

✓  We Are

Direct — say what you mean, no fluff
Confident — we do great work and we know it
Local — Raleigh-first, community-proud
Results-focused — show the outcome, not the process
Approachable — premium but never pretentious
Honest — real prices, real guarantees, no games

✗  We Are Not

Corporate or stiff — this isn't a Fortune 500
Cheap or desperate — never lead with discounts
Vague — "quality service" means nothing, show it
Pushy — urgency should feel real, not manufactured
Over-hyped — let results speak louder than claims
Generic — we serve Raleigh, not "your area"
