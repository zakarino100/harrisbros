## 1. MARKET OPPORTUNITY & POSITIONING

### The Problem You're Solving

**The Core Pain:**
- Home services contractors pay $500–2,000/mo to run Facebook Lead Ads
- Leads go cold within 5-15 minutes if not contacted immediately
- Reps are overwhelmed, inconsistent, or asleep when leads come in (especially evenings/weekends)
- Industry data: 78% of customers choose the first responder. Response within 5 minutes = 21x more likely to qualify
- Most contractors respond in hours, not minutes. Average response time: 42 minutes to 4+ hours
- Result: They're throwing away 60–80% of their lead quality by being slow

**The Math They Understand:**
- Contractor spending: $1,500/mo on Facebook ads
- CPL: $15–30 per lead
- Conversion rate (current, with slow follow-up): 5.2%
- 100 leads/mo × 5.2% = 5.2 jobs/mo
- ROI: Barely breaks even or negative

**With Swell:**
- Same $1,500/mo ad spend, same 100 leads
- Instant AI follow-up (within 60 seconds)
- Conversion rate improves to 12–18% (with fast, consistent follow-up)
- 100 leads/mo × 15% (conservative) = 15 jobs/mo
- 3X more jobs from the same ad spend
- Or: 15% improvement in CPL efficiency = $250–300/mo savings
- **The Pitch:** "You're already spending $1,500/mo on ads. We make those ads 3x more effective by responding faster than 99% of your competitors."

### Why Swell Wins

**vs GoHighLevel:**
- GHL is $100–300/mo software + their lead gen costs + their ad management. Swell is a retainer that includes everything.
- GHL requires the contractor to manage their own SMS sequences. Swell's Hayden AI does it for you.
- GHL is a horizontal platform (CRM, email, SMS, funnels). Swell is vertical: optimized for home services + Facebook Lead Ads specifically.
- Swell has Discord integration (where most agencies already live) — GHL doesn't.

**vs Other "AI Home Services" Tools:**
- Most competitors are lead generation platforms (like ZipRecruiter for home services). Swell is a lead *follow-up* specialist.
- Swell works with leads the contractor already has (Facebook), not replacing their lead source.
- Pre-trained on home services language, quotes, objection handling. Not generic AI.

**vs Internal Hiring/Training:**
- "You want to hire someone to respond to texts 24/7? That's $30–40k/year + benefits. Swell is $1,200/mo and never sleeps."

### Positioning Statement

**For the contractor (your customer):**
"Swell is the AI salesman for your Facebook leads. While your competitors are sleeping, Swell qualifies, quotes, and closes your leads via text — then hands off the hot ones to your rep in Discord. You get 3x the jobs from the same ad budget."

**For the market (ads, content):**
"Your Facebook Lead Ads are broken — not the ads, the follow-up. Swell fixes it."

### Lead Form Questions

**Objective:** Qualify for interest AND get contact info for both immediate outreach and retargeting.

```
1. "What home service do you offer?" (Dropdown)
   Options: Pressure washing, Window cleaning, HVAC, Plumbing, Handyman, 
   Lawn care, Roofing, Gutters, Other

2. "Are you currently running Facebook Lead Ads?" (Yes/No)

3. "What's your biggest challenge with lead follow-up?" (Short text)
   [This gives you insight + personalization hooks]

4. "What's your approximate Facebook ad budget?" (Range)
   Options: <$500/mo, $500–1500, $1500–3000, $3000+

5. Name, Email, Phone, Business Name (standard)

6. "Best time to chat?" (Single choice)
   Options: This week, Next week, Just curious for now
```

**Why these questions:**
- Q1: Qualify vertical (pressure washing, window cleaning, etc. convert best)
- Q2: Qualification gate (no ads = wrong person)
- Q3: Personalization for follow-up + content angle
- Q4: Budget qualifier (if <$500, lower priority; if $3k+, white glove treatment)
- Q5: Contact data
- Q6: Sales signal (urgency indicator)

**Why this works:**
- They see Swell in action immediately

- Disqualifies unserious leads in the first 10 seconds

**If "YES":**
4. Hayden: "Perfect. Let me send you a 3-minute video showing exactly how it works [VIDEO LINK]. Watch it, and I'll have a real person (Jake or whoever) follow up in 24 hours to answer questions. Fair?"

**If "NO" or no response after 3 follow-ups:**
5. Removed from active sequence, added to content/nurture track

**If "YES" + watches video:**
6. Calendar link: "You in for a quick 15-min call with Jake Thursday or Friday? 👇 [CALENDLY]"

## 4. OUTBOUND/COLD OUTREACH STRATEGY

**Goal:** Direct, personalized outreach to high-probability buyers in your target verticals.

### Channel & Sequencing

**Primary: LinkedIn DMs** (Start here)
- Highest deliverability, least spammy, easiest to personalize
- Home services owners check LinkedIn
- 1–1 feel (more likely to respond than email)

**Secondary: Cold Email** (Backup)
- For those who don't have LinkedIn or who ignore DMs
- Slightly more "salesy" but can be personalized

**Tertiary: Cold Calls** (High touch, low volume)
- Only for hot leads (referred, engaged, responded before)
- Skip this initially unless you have super-targeted lists

### Targeting: Where to Find Your Buyers

**Lists to Build:**

1. **Local Service Ads + Facebook Lead Ads** (Your Ideal Customer)
   - Use tools: Hunter.io, RocketReach, Apollo.io
   - Search: "Pressure washing company [CITY]" on Google Maps
   - Pull: Business name, owner name, website, email, phone
   - Filter: Companies with 2–20 employees (sweet spot)
   - Verify LinkedIn profile exists

2. **Facebook Group Lists**
   - Groups: "Pressure Washing Business Owners," "Window Cleaning Contractors," "HVAC Contractors," etc.
   - Scrape member names (or manually if small group)
   - Look up on LinkedIn, add to sequence

3. **Industry Lists**
   - Thumbtack top-rated providers (pressure washing, window cleaning)
   - HomeAdvisor verified contractors
   - Pull their names, search LinkedIn

4. **Competitor Customers**
   - Use sites like Semrush, Ahrefs to see who advertises in your space
   - Look at Google Ads competitors
   - Find their retargeting audiences (Facebook Pixel Hunter tool)
   - Reach out to their customers with "better offer"

5. **Cold Search + LinkedIn Sales Navigator**
   - Use Navigator to find: Job title = Owner, Industry = Home Services, Company size = 2–20, Location = US
   - You'll get 1,000s of people. Quality varies but high volume.
